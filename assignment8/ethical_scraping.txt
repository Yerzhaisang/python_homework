English Wikipedia robots.txt
https://en.wikipedia.org/robots.txt

Which sections are restricted for crawling?

The default rules restrict /w/, /api/, /trap/, and Special pages, including
encoded versions of the Special namespace. They also restrict specific
community and administrative pages, such as article deletion discussions,
copyright problems, protected titles, the spam blacklist, arbitration, and
requests for adminship. The file lists limited exceptions for resources such
as the sitemap and certain API and resource-loader URLs. Ordinary article
pages are generally available to crawlers following the default rules.

Are there rules for specific user agents?

Yes. MJ12bot, UbiCrawler, and wget are disallowed from crawling the entire
site. SemrushBot has a five-second crawl delay. Wikipedia work bots IsraBot
and Orthogaffe each have an empty Disallow directive in their own groups.

Reflection

Robots.txt gives website operators a standard way to tell automated crawlers
which URLs they may request and, in some cases, how frequently they should
make requests. Checking and following those rules helps scrapers respect the
site operator's preferences and limit unnecessary server load.
